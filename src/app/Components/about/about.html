Our story

Built for Georgia,
by Georgians

We started RealEstate Georgia in 2016 with one simple goal — to make finding a home in Georgia as easy and transparent as possible.

Back then, property search was scattered across unreliable classifieds with outdated listings and no way to verify what you were looking at. We built a better way.

Today we serve thousands of buyers, renters and sellers across Tbilisi, Batumi, Rustavi and beyond — with every listing verified, every agent vetted, and every transaction supported by our team.
